What is SmartPath AI? ✨

SmartPath AI is an intelligent learning assistant designed to make studying easier and smarter. It helps students create personalized study plans, manage their time, choose subjects, and build better learning habits.

Instead of confusing schedules, SmartPath AI guides students step by step with a simple and organized learning journey.
